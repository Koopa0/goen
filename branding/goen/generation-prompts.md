# goen icon exploration prompts

These prompts were used with Codex's built-in image generation workflow. Every
output was requested as one centered black icon on a white 1:1 canvas, with no
text, letters, gradients, shadows, texture, 3D, mockup, or literal commerce
pictograms.

## Meeting Loop

Create a compact original abstract mark from one continuous bold rounded path,
or two rounded paths about to meet. Express discovery, meaningful connection,
and ongoing exchange. Keep a strong silhouette at 16 px. Avoid coins, disks,
letters, infinity signs, chain links, handshakes, carts, bags, and boxes.

## Open Portal

Create two opposing, slightly offset rounded geometric forms with a clear
negative-space passage between them. Express an open entrance, two parties
meeting, and value moving through a platform. Keep the result abstract,
balanced, and suitable for a modern ecommerce technology brand.

## Exchange Tiles

Create a single compact mark from two or three rounded modules that align,
overlap, or share a boundary. Suggest products, buyers, and merchants
participating in one platform without depicting UI cards, a package, arrows,
or a storefront.

## Flow Portal refinement

Preserve two opposing solid geometric forms separated by a continuous
S-shaped negative-space channel. Make the overall silhouette compact and close
to square, the two forms optically balanced, the corner radii consistent, and
the channel wide enough to survive at 16 px. Keep it purely geometric and
nonliteral.
