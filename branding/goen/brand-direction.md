# goen — modern mark direction

## Position

`goen` is a modern ecommerce technology brand about helping people encounter
the right products. The Japanese ideas of `ご縁` and `五円` belong in the
brand story, but the visual mark does not need to depict a coin.

## Visual principle

- Express connection through relationship, spacing, rhythm, and motion.
- Prefer an abstract, ownable silhouette over a literal commerce pictogram.
- Keep the mark legible in one color and at 16 px.
- Use real typography for `goen` and `ご縁`; never generate the wordmark as an
  image.
- Avoid coins, currency details, shopping carts, bags, handshakes, chain links,
  infinity symbols, generic arrows, gradients, shadows, and 3D treatments.

## Recommended route: Flow Portal

Two balanced forms are separated by a continuous negative-space path. The
forms represent two sides of a relationship; the open path represents the
platform that lets people, merchants, and products meet.

- Raster exploration: `icon-explorations/modern-flow-refined.png`
- Clean scalable candidate: `goen-flow-mark.svg`
- Preview: `goen-flow-mark-preview.png`

The SVG uses `currentColor`, so its color can be controlled directly by CSS.
It is a candidate for refinement, not a cleared final trademark.

## Accuracy note

The current Japanese five-yen coin has a round central hole, not a square one.
A square opening belongs more broadly to East Asian historical coin imagery.
If that hybrid reference is ever reused, describe it as a cross-cultural
abstraction rather than a literal five-yen coin.
